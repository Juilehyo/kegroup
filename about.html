Our Founders

About Joonwoo Kim

Born in Seoul and raised in Los Angeles, Joonwoo founded K&E in 2019 after a decade in restaurant operations and franchise development across the US and Korea. Before K&E, he led US expansion for a Seoul-based F&B group, opening more than 40 franchise locations in three years. He holds a B.S. from UC Berkeley and an MBA from Wharton, and currently advises two early-stage food-tech startups.

About Hyojeong Kim

Hyojeong grew up between Seoul and Northern Virginia, working weekends at her family's bakery before a career in supply chain and private equity. She joined K&E as Co-Founder and COO in 2020, overseeing store operations, franchise relationships, and the group's growing real estate pipeline. She holds a B.A. from Georgetown and spent six years at a food & beverage-focused investment firm before returning to the industry she grew up in.
