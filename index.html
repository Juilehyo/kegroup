The portfolio

BHC Chicken

Korea's No.1 fried chicken brand since 1997 — 2,230+ stores built on the signature Bburinkle cheese dry rub. BHC is now expanding across the United States, and K&E is part of that story.

Tous les Jours

K&E operates Tous les Jours bakery cafés in Virginia — the French-inspired Korean bakery baking fresh through the day: breads, pastries, cakes, and coffee. One of the world's largest bakery café franchises, with more than 1,750 stores worldwide.

Visit Website ↗

SALT Korean BBQ

Open since 2022 in Greater Philadelphia, SALT pairs table-grilled USDA Prime beef with curated specialty salts. A second, ground-up flagship is on the way — dry-aged meats on display, a wine vault, private dining, and a stone-carved bar.

Visit Website ↗

Mammoth Cafe

Born in Hongdae, Seoul in 2012, Mammoth grew past 1,000 stores on a simple promise: quality coffee and desserts at a price you don't think twice about. Its first Dubai location is about to open, taking the brand beyond Korea.

From bakery cafés and premium Korean BBQ to Korea's most-loved chicken and coffee brands, K&E is an essential ingredient in the future of K-food.
